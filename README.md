## Research

### DirectFisheye-GS · CVPR 2026 Highlight

**Enabling Native Fisheye Input in Gaussian Splatting with Cross-View Joint Optimization**

Zhengxian Yang\*, **Fei Xie\***, et al. · \* Equal contribution

DirectFisheye-GS brings native fisheye inputs to 3D Gaussian Splatting for wide-field-of-view reconstruction. Cross-view joint optimization strengthens geometric and photometric consistency while reducing information loss from undistortion and artifacts near image boundaries.

**My contributions:** core algorithm development, implementation of the complete training pipeline, literature review, and manuscript writing.

[Project Page](https://yzxqh.github.io/DirectFisheye-GS/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00648) · [CVPR Proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Yang_DirectFisheye-GS_Enabling_Native_Fisheye_Input_in_Gaussian_Splatting_with_Cross-View_CVPR_2026_paper.html) · [Code](https://github.com/Metaverse-AI-Lab-THU/DirectFisheye-GS)
